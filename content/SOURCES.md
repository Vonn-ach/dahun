## Earlier Kayan seed

Dahun started with a draft Kayan (`kys`) seed compiled from written sources: C. H. Southwell's *Kayan–English Dictionary* (reference only), R. Blust's ABVD wordlist, Borneo Dictionary, blogs by Kayan speakers (paren nyawi; Marriane Tang), Dr Roselind Wan's Kayan Language Legacy Project, and a few single terms from other public pages. The course moved to Kenyah, the owner's mother tongue (decision D016). The seed and its full source list are kept in the git tag `kayan-seed-v1`, ready for a Kayan community partner.
