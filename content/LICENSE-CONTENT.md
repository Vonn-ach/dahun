# Content licence

**The language content in this folder (`content/`), including words, phrases, sentences, notes and audio recordings, is NOT covered by the MIT licence that applies to the code.**

Status: **to be agreed with the Kayan community and the contributing speakers.** Until then:

- All rights in speaker contributions (corrections, new entries, recordings) remain with the speakers and their community. They are used in this app with their consent.
- Material derived from third-party sources keeps those sources' terms. See `SOURCES.md`.
- You may view this content to understand or contribute to the project. Please do not copy it into other apps, datasets or AI training sets without permission. Open an issue to ask.

## Options under discussion

1. **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**: free for non-commercial use with credit, and derivatives must share alike.
2. **Traditional Knowledge (TK) labels from Local Contexts** (localcontexts.org) on top of a licence, to state community rules (e.g. "attribution", "non-commercial", "seasonal" or "community use only" for specific cultural items).
3. **A custom community licence** written with community representatives.

When a decision is made, record it in `docs/DECISIONS.md` and replace this file.
